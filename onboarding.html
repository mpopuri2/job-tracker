Welcome to Job Tracker

Save job applications to your Google Drive in one click

1

Pin to toolbar

Click the puzzle 🧩 icon in Chrome's toolbar → find Job Tracker → click the 📌 pin icon to keep it visible.

2

Open any job posting

Visit a job on LinkedIn, Indeed, Workday, Greenhouse, or any other site.

3

Click the Job Tracker icon in the toolbar

The extension automatically extracts the job title, company, location, and full description.

4

Sign in with Google & log it

Your data saves to Job Application Files/ in your Google Drive — only you can see it.

🔒 Your data stays yours

Everything saves directly to your personal Google Drive. We have zero access to your job data, resume files, or any information you enter. No servers, no databases — just your Drive.
